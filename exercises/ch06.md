Source: *Linear Algebra: One Language, Many Dialects*, draft manuscript (October 2026).

## Exercises

### E06.01 — Computational

Over $\mathbb{F}_2$, calculate the polar form of $Q(x,y)=x^2+xy+y^2$ and list its zeros.

**Companion status:** Runnable worked instance.

Check result: `la-sensei chapter 6` → `E06.01`.

### E06.02 — Structural

Prove that a finite-dimensional nondegenerate alternating form has even-dimensional underlying space, in every characteristic.

**Companion status:** Reader proof / discussion / exploration.

**Suggested method:** Give the abstract argument first; if computing examples, state which additional assumptions you used.

### E06.03 — Conceptual

Explain why $A^\mathsf{T}MA$ classifies forms but $A^{-1}MA$ classifies operators.

### E06.04 — Computational

### E06.05 — Challenge
