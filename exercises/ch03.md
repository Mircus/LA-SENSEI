Source: *Linear Algebra: One Language, Many Dialects*, draft manuscript (October 2026).

## Exercises

### E03.01 — Computational

Verify $A^3=I$ for the opening matrix, and find its eigenvalues over $\mathbb{F}_4$.

**Companion status:** Runnable worked instance.

### E03.02 — Conceptual

Give two operators over $\mathbb{F}_2$ with the same characteristic polynomial $(x+1)^2$, one diagonalizable and one not.

**Companion status:** Runnable worked instance.

Check result: `la-sensei chapter 3` → `E03.02`.

### E03.03 — Structural

Prove that a polynomial with pairwise distinct roots has a diagonalizable companion matrix over its splitting field.

**Companion status:** Reader proof / discussion / exploration.

**Suggested method:** Give the abstract argument first; if computing examples, state which additional assumptions you used.

### E03.04 — Exploratory

Over $\mathbb{F}_3$, investigate the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$. Does it acquire eigenvalues over $\mathbb{F}_9$? What is its order?

**Companion status:** Runnable worked instance.

Check result: `la-sensei chapter 3` → `E03.04`.

### E03.05 — Challenge

Explain why finite fields are perfect: show that irreducible polynomials over $\mathbb{F}_q$ have no repeated roots in an algebraic closure.

**Companion status:** Reader proof / discussion / exploration.

**Suggested method:** Give the abstract argument first; if computing examples, state which additional assumptions you used.
